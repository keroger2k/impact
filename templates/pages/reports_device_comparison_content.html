Device Inventory Comparison

Side-by-side reconciliation of Catalyst Center's device inventory against SolarWinds'. DNAC comes from its cached inventory; SolarWinds is queried live for Cisco nodes. Devices are matched on hostname and management IP together. Nexus and ACI are dropped from the SolarWinds side (Catalyst Center doesn't manage them, so they'd always read as gaps), and wireless is dropped from both — everything filtered is listed under Excluded.
